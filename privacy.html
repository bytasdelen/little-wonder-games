So Many Cats! Privacy Policy

SO MANY CATS! — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. About this policy
This policy explains how information is handled in connection with So Many Cats!, a single-player organizing game developed and published under the Little Wonder Games name. Contact: littlewondergames@hotmail.com.

2. Local gameplay data
The game stores progress, settings and other gameplay data on your device so you can continue playing. This includes your collection and shop progress and your chosen settings. Local files are not automatically sent to a developer-operated gameplay server. You can remove local data using the game's available controls or by deleting the relevant local save files; deleting saves may permanently remove progress. A store platform may separately synchronize data if you enable a supported platform feature.

3. Store accounts and ownership verification
The Steam and Epic versions use their respective platform services to authenticate the player and verify ownership of the game. The game may process a platform account identifier, authentication tokens and an ownership result for this purpose. We do not ask for your Steam or Epic account password.

For Epic Account Services, Epic's required Basic Profile permission allows access to your account ID, display name, language and linked account display names. Ownership verification uses your Epic account and the game's catalog item identifier. The game does not request friends-list, online-presence or country permissions. Authentication and ownership requests are sent to the relevant platform. Network services may process connection information, including your IP address, as part of serving those requests. This information is used to provide access to the purchased game, rather than advertising or profiling.

4. Support messages
If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information. Diagnostic files or screenshots should only be shared when you choose to provide them. Support information is kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

5. Advertising, analytics and purchases
The current single-player game has no in-game advertising, behavioral advertising, in-game purchases, chat or user-generated-content service. We do not sell players' personal information. The game does not send gameplay analytics to a developer-operated analytics service. Store operators may independently collect store, purchase, usage or diagnostic information under their own policies.

6. Third-party services
Steam is operated by Valve and Epic Games Store / Epic Account Services are operated by Epic Games. They handle platform accounts, purchases and their own service information under their respective privacy policies:
Epic Games: https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/privacy-policy
Steam / Valve: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/
This policy is also available on Google Docs; Google processes visits to that document under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor connection information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation under its privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics scripts.

7. Your choices and requests
You can manage platform privacy settings through your Steam or Epic account. For information about platform-held data, contact the relevant platform. For information you have sent directly to us, email littlewondergames@hotmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on your location, you may have other privacy rights, including a right to object to certain processing or complain to your local data protection authority. We respond in accordance with applicable law. You may delete local save data yourself; platform synchronization or backups may need to be managed separately.

8. Younger players
The game does not ask players to enter a real name, date of birth or contact details inside the game. Platform account and parental-consent requirements are managed by the relevant platform. Parents or guardians with a privacy question can contact us at the address above.

9. Changes and contact
We may update this policy when the game or relevant services change. The updated date appears at the top. For privacy questions about So Many Cats!, contact Little Wonder Games at littlewondergames@hotmail.com.
